# Research: Consonant Spectral Signatures

## 1. Research Findings: Consonants
- **S (Sibilant)**: 
    - Frequencies: High energy > 5000Hz (typically 5kHz - 10kHz).
    - Nature: Continuous white noise.
    - Filter: High-pass.
- **SH (Sibilant)**:
    - Frequencies: 2000Hz - 5000Hz. Lower than 'S'.
    - Nature: Turbulent noise.
    - Filter: Band-pass.
- **T (Plosive)**:
    - Frequencies: Broad spectrum burst > 3000Hz.
    - Nature: Transient "click" followed by short noise burst.
    - Decay: Rapid (< 40ms).
- **P (Plosive)**:
    - Frequencies: Low-frequency "thud" < 200Hz + weak mid-range noise burst.
    - Nature: Labial closure release.

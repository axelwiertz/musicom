Arnold cat map - Scholarpedia www.scholarpedia.org/article/Arnold's_cat_map The Arnold Cat map is an area-preserving map on the two-dimensional torus defined by (x′ y′) = (2 1 1 1)(x y) mod 1. It was introduced by Vladimir Arnold and he used a sketch of a cat to illustrate the chaotic properties of this map . It is an example of an Anosov diffeomorphism. References Arnold , V. I. and A.

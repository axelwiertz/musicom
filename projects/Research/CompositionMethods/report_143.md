### Source

- **Wikipedia: "Fokker periodicity block"** (rev. 2025-10-18; fetched 2026-11-15, cached `_web143/74025fbe.bin` → `fpb_wiki.txt`) — the canonical definition used here: just ratios as points on a prime-factor lattice; **unison vectors** = small lattice intervals (commas); treating pitches separated by a comma as equivalent *folds the lattice* — "mathematically, this corresponds to finding the quotient group of the original lattice by the sublattice generated by the commas"; $n$ linearly independent commas in an $n$-dimensional lattice → a finite abelian group = the **periodicity block**; "Frequently, it forms a cyclic group, in which case identifying the $m$ pitches of the periodicity block with $m$-equal tuning gives equal tuning approximations of the just ratios that defined the original lattice." The tile area/volume = $|\det U|$ of the unison-vector matrix = number of nodes in the block. Worked examples: 12 (commas 128/125 + 81/80), 19 (81/80 + 3125/3072), 31 (81/80 + 225/224 + 1029/1024). ### Paradigm

**Rules-Based** — the block, its size, its scale, its progression graph and its tension curve all follow from deterministic lattice algebra: determinants of unison-vector matrices, quotients of free abelian groups, coset enumeration, exact rational arithmetic. There is no stochastic sampling, no nature-inspired dynamics, and no learned/neural parameter — the same relationship 131 TDRPC has to ratio arithmetic or 065 TTSMC to the serial matrix. ### Description

**Fokker Periodicity Block Composition (FPBC)** composes from Adriaan Fokker's periodicity blocks: the finite scale that results when you *fold a just-intonation lattice by its commas*. This is the tuning-theoretic mirror of 131 TDRPC: the tonality diamond asks "which ratios, bounded by an odd limit?"; the periodicity block asks "which ratios survive quotienting the whole lattice by the near-unisons we agree to ignore?"

**The lattice.** Fix a prime limit — 5-limit for the classic 2-dimensional case, 7-limit for Fokker's own 3-dimensional organ tuning. Pitch classes are points of the free abelian group $\mathbb{Z}^n$: a point $(a,b,c)$ is the octave-reduced ratio

$$r = \mathrm{oct}_2\!\left(3^a\, 5^b\, 7^c\right), \qquad \mathrm{oct}_2: \mathbb{R}_{>0} \to [1,2),$$

with the 2-axis reserved as the octave-reduction coordinate (octave equivalence is assumed throughout, exactly as the Wikipedia article states). Moving one step along an axis multiplies by a prime interval: the 5-limit lattice is the familiar Tonnetz of perfect fifths $(1,0)$ and just major thirds $(0,1)$.

**The quotient.** Choose $n$ **unison vectors** $u_1,\dots,u_n \in \mathbb{Z}^n$ — small intervals (commas) that the composition agrees to treat as equivalent to the unison, e.g. the syntonic comma $81/80 = (4,-1,0)$ ($3^4 \cdot 5^{-1}$, 21.5 cents) or the diesis $128/125 = (0,-3,0)$ (41.1 cents). These span a sublattice $U = \langle u_1,\dots,u_n\rangle \subseteq \mathbb{Z}^n$; the **periodicity block** is a set of coset representatives of the quotient

$$\mathcal{B}(U) \subset \mathbb{Z}^n / U, \qquad |\mathcal{B}(U)| = |\det U|,$$

i.e. exactly $|\det U|$ pitch classes (the tile area/volume theorem of the source). Concretely, my verifier enumerates $\mathcal{B}(U)$ by the Wikipedia membership criterion with origin anchor: $p \in \mathcal{B}(U) \iff \lfloor p \cdot U^{-1} \rfloor = \mathbf{0}$ — the lattice points in the half-open fundamental parallelepiped $U \cdot [0,1)^n$, computed in exact `Fraction` arithmetic.

**Cyclicity = the tuning map.** The block "shows how $m$-equal tuning approximates the 5-limit" because the equal-temperament mapping *is* the quotient map: define $\varphi: \mathbb{Z}^n \to \mathbb{Z}_m$ by $p \mapsto \sum_i p_i \lfloor m\log_2(\text{prime}_i) \rceil \bmod m$ (e.g. $12$-ET: $\varphi(a,b) = 19a + 28b \bmod 12$; $31$-ET: $49a + 72b + 87c \bmod 31$). Verified for all three blocks: (i) every unison vector lies in $\ker\varphi$; (ii) the block's $m$ members map bijectively onto $\mathbb{Z}_m$. Both index-$m$ subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}^n$ are then equal — $U = \ker\varphi$ — hence $\mathbb{Z}^n/U \cong \mathbb{Z}_m$ **cyclic**, and $\varphi$ is the quotient map. (Sanity: gcd of all unison-vector entries $=1$ for all three, i.e. the Smith-normal-form first invariant $d_1=1$; the blocks are not split into product groups. A non-cyclic block, e.g. $\mathbb{Z}_2\oplus\mathbb{Z}_6$, is possible with badly chosen commas — Pitfall 7.)

**Periodicity and the comma pump.** The block *tiles the lattice*: translating the block by any unison vector returns to the same quotient class — verified: $\mathcal{B} + (4,-1)$ has the identical 12-ET pc set (the "periodicity" that gives the blocks their name and makes the reprise a theorem, exactly as 141 ACMTPC's cat-map period does). But the *just ratios* do not return: translating by $81/80$ multiplies every ratio by the comma — measured **0 shared exact ratios** between $\mathcal{B}$ and $\mathcal{B}+(4,-1)$ (all 12 shift by 21.5 cents). This is the **comma pump** — enharmonic equivalence in the temperament, real drift in just intonation. It is the method's TENSE engine: a section can walk the same tempered scale while the just realization slides by one comma per unison-vector step, accumulating microtonal tension that resolves only when the drift returns to a unison (the pump closes after cycling through the block's group structure).

**Progression = block transposition.** Transposing the block by a *non*-unison lattice vector $t$ (e.g. a fifth $(1,0)$ or a third $(0,1)$) is a modulation to a different coset class. Measured seams on the 12-block (exact ratio overlap): $+$fifth → **8/12** common ratios, $+$third → **8/12**, $+15/8$ (the $(1,1)$ diagonal) → **5/12**. The overlap is the concrete layer's common-tone voice-leading material, the lattice analogue of 131 TDRPC's identity-adjacency seams.

**Abstract procedure** (what this registration adds): (1) pick the prime limit $n$ (5-limit = classic 2-D; 7-limit = Fokker's 3-D organ tuning); (2) choose $n$ unison vectors — start from the verified triples above, or compose custom commas and compute $|\det U|$; (3) enumerate the block (membership criterion, exact arithmetic) and sort it → the scale + step pattern; (4) assign a **block schedule** per section: which block (unison-vector set), which transposition $t_j$, which register; (5) compute the seam obligations (common-tone ratio sets between adjacent sections) and the tension curve (comma-pump count per section, limit drift); (6) hand the plan — per-section ratio subsets, per-voice block layers, seam common-tone constraints, comma-pump curve — to `rules/subset_network.py`.

### Musical Elements Framework

- **PITCH**: the block *is* the pitch vocabulary — $|\det U|$ octave-reduced just ratios (12/19/31 verified above), each an exact frequency ratio with an exact cent value; pitch identity is a ratio, not a semitone. Melodic motion = steps of the sorted block (measured 12-block steps 70.7–133.2 cents — an *unequal* chromatic: the temperament's justness lives in those inequalities) vs lattice leaps (block transpositions). Contour follows the block's order; enharmonic identity is the comma pump, not 12-TET unison.
- **RHYTHM**: the abstract layer fixes no rhythm — it constrains it: block cardinality is the natural cycle length (12/19/31-note rotations of "which block member is active now"), the step pattern's large/small alternation is a ready-made metric cell (the 133.2/70.7-cent alternation of the 12-block is a rhythmically usable unequal pulse), and the comma-pump count per section is an energy envelope (each pump = one microtonal tension increment). Concrete realization quantizes to the tick grid; the block's unequal steps survive only in pitch, not in time (the rhythm layer is 12-TET-grid-locked as usual — FPBC's microtonality is a pitch phenomenon).
- **HARMONY**: vertical chords are *slices of the block*: adjacent block members stacked = the temperament's chords (in the 12-block, the 6/5–5/4 region carries the just major/minor thirds; in the 31-block, the 7-limit ratios give Fokker's septimal sonorities). Progression = block transposition (measured seams 8/12, 8/12, 5/12 common ratios = smooth voice leading); cadence = return to the origin coset (block + unison vector = the same tempered chord, comma-pumped). The unison $1/1$ is the HOME nexus, exactly as in 131's diamond.
- **STRUCTURE**: macro-form = the **block schedule**: section $j$ = (unison-vector set $U_j$, transposition $t_j$, register). The determinant selects the section's scale size (12 = diatonic-chromatic 12-ET world; 19 = meantone with more commas; 31 = Fokker's 7-limit world); the transposition sequence $t_1,\dots,t_S$ is the key scheme; the comma-pump count is the tension arc (HOME = few pumps near the origin, TENSE = many pumps away, LIFT = transposition to a bright transposition, TURN = pump closure back to the origin coset — the periodicity that makes reprise a theorem); changing the unison-vector set between sections = a temperament change (a genuine modulatory event).
- **TEXTURE**: voices = *layers of the same block*: bass voice takes the low block members, lead walks the step pattern, inner voices take transposed copies at different registers (the block is a pc pool; register is a per-voice octave placement decision, as in 131 Pitfall 7). Block cardinality controls density ceiling (a 31-block section can carry 31 distinct pcs before repetition); multiple simultaneous transpositions = polytonal stratification (block + fifth over block + root). The unequal step pattern gives the texture a microtonal "grain" that a flattened 12-TET rendering erases.

### UnitMatrix Integration (Voices & Sections)

**Voices = rows = block layers.** Row $v$ carries: a subset of the section's block (e.g. bass = members 0–3, lead = the full step pattern, pad = a transposed copy), a register band (octave placement of its block members), and a role (nexus holder, comma-pump walker, transposition carrier). Rows inside a section draw only from that section's block (vertical coherence, like voices of one tuning); rows transposed by different $t$ = deliberate bitonality within one temperament. The abstract plan per row: its ratio subset + register + seam common-tone obligations.

**Sections = columns = block regions.** Column $j$ = (prime limit $n_j$, unison-vector set $U_j$, transposition $t_j$, target register): the column's pool is the block $\mathcal{B}(U_j) + t_j$. The transposition sequence is the key scheme; the comma-pump count per column is the tension curve; the seam rule is common-tone retention: the ratio set of column $j$'s last cell must intersect column $j{+}1$'s first (measured overlaps above give the concrete voice-leading obligations — the analogue of 033 WFCGS seam consistency and 131's identity-adjacency seams, but in quotient space: the seam is a *coset relation*, not a shared scale degree). A temperament change between columns (new $U_j$) is a macro-form event.

**Cells = MusicUnit (abstract form).** Cell $(v,j)$ = row $v$'s role realization inside column $j$'s block — as an abstract object: a ratio subset with role labels, a target register, and comma-pump obligations at both seams. The concrete layer converts it: ratio → frequency (pitch-bend or a just renderer), block member → scale-degree-snapped pitch event, step pattern → rhythmic cell — then `create_note_unit`/`_unit_from_events` fills the cell and `validate()` gates zero-drift (the abstract plan itself emits no events, so it cannot drift).

### Pitfalls

1. **The name collides with Fokker-Planck.** `grep fokker` on the DB before entry returns 2 hits, both *Fokker–Planck equation* prose in 107 SDEC — the physicist's diffusion equation, not Adriaan Fokker the tuning theorist. Never write "Fokker" unqualified in this DB; the equation and the periodicity blocks are different Fokkers (the same man, two legacies — the Wikipedia bio lists both as "Known for").
2. **The block content is anchor-dependent.** The *representative set* depends on the origin offset $(x_0,y_0)$ of the membership criterion (the source says: choose it "preferably not a node"). The origin-anchored 12-block is $\{1/1, 27/25, 144/125, \dots\}$; the standard 5-limit chromatic $\{1/1, 16/15, 9/8, 6/5, 5/4, 4/3, 45/32, \dots\}$ is a *different transversal of the same quotient* — both verified to map bijectively onto the same 12 pcs. What is invariant is the quotient, the pc set under the temperament, the step-pattern *multiset* — not the exact representative ratios. Document the anchoring; never claim one fixed ratio list is "the" block.
3. **12-TET projection destroys the method.** Nearest-semitone mapping collapses distinct just ratios onto shared semitones (measured 12-block steps: 70.7, 92.2, 111.7, 133.2 cents — all distinct) and voids the comma pump, the common-tone arithmetic and the constant-structure property. The abstract layer keeps ratios; realization must use per-note pitch-bend, a just renderer, or at minimum record exact cents in provenance. A 12-TET sketch is a *reduction*, not the method (same rule as 131 Pitfall 2).
4. **Determinant discipline.** Block size is $|\det U|$ — absolute value, and the vectors must be linearly independent ($\det \neq 0$). With fewer unison vectors than lattice dimensions you get a *temperament with degrees of freedom* (a continuum of blocks, not one block) — that is legitimate but different: pick $n$ vectors for a closed block, or say explicitly that the remaining dimensions stay untempered.
5. **Octave equivalence is assumed, not optional.** The 2-axis is the reduction coordinate; the block lives in $\mathbb{Z}^n / U$ mod octave. If you want non-octave blocks (Stretched/Split octave systems), you must add 2 as a real lattice axis and a fourth unison vector in 3-limit-plus-2 space — a different construction; don't mix the two.
6. **Comma pump ≠ modulation.** Translating by a unison vector leaves the tempered pc set *unchanged* (sounds like the same pitches — verified: identical 12-ET pc set) while drifting the just ratios by the comma (verified: 0 shared exact ratios). Use it as *enharmonic tension* (a pump that closes on the cycle), never as a key change; key changes are non-unison transpositions (seams measured 5–8/12 common ratios).
7. **Cyclicity is not automatic.** The m-ET mapping is only a well-defined quotient map when $U \subseteq \ker\varphi$ (constant-structure-preserving); badly chosen commas can give non-cyclic quotients ($\mathbb{Z}_2 \oplus \mathbb{Z}_6$ instead of $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$) that still have $|\det|=12$ but cannot be identified with 12-ET without collisions. Verify with the kernel-identity check (unison vectors in $\ker\varphi$ + block bijects onto $\mathbb{Z}_m$) — the verifier's check group [4] — before claiming "block $\approx m$-ET".
8. **Limit inflation.** Higher limits mean more unison vectors (5-limit needs 2, 7-limit needs 3, 11-limit needs 5) and tiny commas → determinants explode; a 5-vector 11-limit block can easily exceed a hundred pitch classes. Start from the verified triples (12/19/31); use 11-limit only for deliberate microtonal sprawl (same caution as 131 Pitfall 9).
9. **The unequal step pattern is the feature, not a bug.** The 12-block's steps are not 100 cents (measured 70.7–133.2); flattening them to equal semitones erases the temperament's justness and the constant-structure property that ties ratio identity to step count. If a section must sound "normal", pick a *different block* (e.g. the 31-block contains near-diatonic regions), don't quantize the ratios.
10. **Neighbour audit.** 131 TDRPC = bounded odd-limit *identity pool* + otonality/utonality hexads (no quotient, no temperament); 114 FSBC = unbounded Farey/Stern–Brocot *rational tree* for rhythm (no commas); 025 Sieve = modular congruence sets (cosets of cyclic $\mathbb{Z}_m$, not multi-dimensional sublattices); 069 CWCC = well-formed Christoffel scales (Sturmian step patterns, no lattice folding); 113 PCFSC = Fourier sets over $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$ (12-TET fixed). FPBC is the first *lattice-quotient / temperament* method in the DB; repo antecedent: none — `rules/fokker_blocks.py` is new (the existing `rules/set_theory.py` normal-form/ICV kernel is the pitch-class sibling, but has no ratio-lattice machinery).

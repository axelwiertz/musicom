## 1. Executive Summary & One-Line Description

**Scalar Auxiliary Variable Nonlinear String Synthesis (SAV-NSS)** simulates large-amplitude, geometrically exact nonlinear transverse string vibration coupled to multi-modal bridge admittance and acoustic radiation filters via the Scalar Auxiliary Variable (SAV) quadratisation approach. The non-negative nonlinear elastic potential is transformed into an auxiliary scalar variable and a spatial gradient vector field, allowing the coupled discrete-time equations of motion to be inverted explicitly via two sequential Sherman–Morrison rank-one updates in $\mathcal{O}(M + J)$ operations per sample. It reproduces dynamic pitch glide, overtone enrichment, phantom partials, and body wood warmth without costly iterative solvers or matrix factorizations.

## 5. Technical Mechanics Summary

1. **Geometrically Exact String Model:** Retains pointwise Green–Lagrange strain $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{GL}} = \sqrt{1 + \zeta^2} - 1$ from local slope $\zeta = \partial_x u$ without neglecting transverse-longitudinal coupling effects.
2. **SAV Quadratisation:** Introduces scalar variable $\psi(t) \triangleq \sqrt{2\phi_{\mathrm{nl}}(u) + \varepsilon}$, decomposing nonlinear elastic forces into $\psi(t) g(x,t)$.
3. **Modal Expansion with Nonlinear Cap:** Truncates string into $M$ modes, evaluating nonlinear interactions only on the lower $M_{\mathrm{nl}}$ modes ($f \le 5\text{ kHz}$), reducing quadrature costs while preserving high-frequency linear shimmer.
4. **Dual Sherman–Morrison Inversion:** Formulates the discrete-time state update as two sequential rank-one perturbations of a diagonal system matrix $\mathbf{D}$:
   - Update 1: Inverts the bilateral bridge coupling $\mathbf{f}\tilde{\mathbf{v}}^\top$ once at initialization.
   - Update 2: Inverts the time-varying SAV nonlinear force term in $\mathcal{O}(M_{\mathrm{nl}} + J)$ floating-point operations per sample.
5. **Servo Drift Regulation:** Corrects the discrete auxiliary variable $\psi^{n-1/2}$ against $\sqrt{2\phi_{\mathrm{nl}}(\boldsymbol{\zeta}^n) + \varepsilon}$ along the velocity displacement vector, preventing long-term numerical accumulation during sustained ring-down.

## 6. Musical Elements Framework

- **PITCH:** Dynamic pitch glide upon attack (10–60 cents sharp at plucking onset, decaying smoothly to nominal fundamental). Euler-Bernoulli bending stiffness ($EI \partial_x^4$) ensures realistic dispersion.
- **RHYTHM:** Microsecond-sharp transient onset matching physical plectrum or fingernail release, followed by two-stage decay (fast body radiation, slow sustain).
- **HARMONY:** Non-stationary timbre and phantom partials generated by mode coupling; open strings undergo sympathetic resonance via the shared bridge constraint force $F_b(t)$.
- **STRUCTURE:** Continuous dynamic velocity response. Low velocities produce clean, linear harp-like tones; forte strikes explode into bright, buzzing nonlinear string bite.
- **TEXTURE:** Multi-string polyphony with bridge interaction provides acoustic air, wood warmth, and authentic guitar/lute presence.

## 7. Quirks, Pitfalls & Mitigations Hit

1. **SAV Drift at Low Sample Rates:** Unregulated SAV drifts over 2+ second plucks at 44.1 kHz. Mitigated via Risse-Hélie servo correction.
2. **Sign-Flipping:** High velocity impulses can cause $\psi$ to oscillate through zero. Mitigated via lower-bound clamp $\psi \ge \sqrt{\varepsilon}$.
3. **Quadrature Complexity:** Calculating slope along the entire spatial continuum is unnecessary. Mitigated by restricting the quadrature grid to $N_g \ge 2 M_{\mathrm{nl}}$ points.
4. **Zero-Drift Compliance:** Audio buffers are zero-padded to match the exact tick length of each UnitMatrix cell.

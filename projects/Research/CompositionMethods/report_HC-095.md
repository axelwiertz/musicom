## 2. The method in one paragraph

A joik is not a song *about* something; it **is** that thing, sung. The Sámi verb *juoigat* is transitive: you joik a person, a wolf, a mountain. In the north most joiks are personal — often made at a person's birth — and function like a name, so the composer's task is **character-capture, not theme-writing**: a two-to-six-note **nucleus** must be recognisable in about two seconds, inside a pentatonic field that the joiker is explicitly licensed to exceed ("joikers are at liberty to use any tones they please"). Rhythm is a **gait** — walking, skiing, rowing, sledge-driving, herding — so phrases are breaths, not bars, and the pulse is unaccented. The form is a **cycle**: state the nucleus, rotate it, ornament it, restate; a joik "can be endless," may be entered and left anywhere, and has no cadence, no development into a contrasting theme, no tonic arrival. Ornamentation is done with **portamento** into and out of structural tones, tense forward placement, minimal vibrato and microtonal sag — the surface is shaped *between* the notes. Text is optional, unrhymed, non-narrative: vocables (*na, lo, ja, vuoi, daja*) chosen for vowel colour, with real words as allusive hooks. Correctness is social: listeners must say *"that's him."*

## 3. Craft procedure (as a human executes it)

1. **Fix the subject, and its character.** Not a plot — a character. Kristoffer Sjulsson (1828–1908): the singer expresses "his conception of a person's or an object's qualities, as well as his own feelings for that person, or the memories stirred when remembering them."
2. **Collect 3–5 character markers** (gait, weight, mood, silhouette, weather) to be encoded in interval choice, contour and rhythmic feel. Nils Anders Påve on the mountain Čokku: *"It shines like a star. Therefore it is joiked."*
3. **Find the nucleus** — 2–6 notes, recognisable in ~2 s, because it *is* the name. Pentatonic default; narrow ambitus (5th–octave) in core *luohti*; South Sámi *vuelie* prefers wide leaps; the tone field is a home, not a law.
4. **Set the gait.** Choose the body motion the joik rides. This fixes pulse density, breath cycle and phrase length before pitch is fixed. No bar, no downbeat accent.
5. **Build the cycle.** Statement → rotation (start on the nucleus's second note) → ornament → restatement. Interval order preserved; rhythm and glide re-shaped. Runs as long as the singer wants; can be entered mid-cycle.
6. **Ornament by glide, not addition.** Portamento into/out of structural tones; long tones sag or brighten microtonally; larynx forward and tense; vibrato minimal. Dialect markers: Inari *llivðe* short lines + nasal; Lule/Pite *vuolle* melodic, narrative, slow; Skolt *leuʹdd* epic, long improvised text, complex rhythm.
7. **Set vocables.** Syllable chain for vowel colour (front brighter/higher, back darker/lower); coded "joik language" syllables; a full narrative libretto is wrong for classic *luohti*.
8. **Check it socially.** Sing it to people who knew the subject; recognition, not beauty, is the test.
9. **Transmit ear-to-ear.** Historically un-notated, learned close-range in work contexts; joiks are inherited/gifted and sometimes retire with the person; recording since Valkeapää (1968) and the DAT label (1970s).
10. **Modern setting (separate craft).** Keep the nucleus intact; add drone/pulse/choir/electronics — Wimme Saari, Mari Boine, Frode Fjellheim.

## 5. UnitMatrix mapping

### Voices (rows)
- **V0 — Joiker.** Nucleus spine; invariant interval order; alone in S0.
- **V1 — Second joiker.** Nucleus delayed/advanced 1–2 beats, more ornament, exaggerated glides. A *variant*, never a chord tone (same logic as HC-094 "seconding", same aesthetic: unblended unison).
- **V2 — Drone / sustained fundamental.** Optional invariant tonic or fifth, or the joik-string's open pitch; supplies the harmonic vacuum.
- **V3 — Gait layer** (ensemble/modern only). Unaccented stride, no backbeat, no swing.
- **V4 — Metadata row.** Dialect tag, subject marker, nucleus ID, glide log, rotation index. Not sounded.

Monophony default: traditional *luohti* = V0 alone. V1–V3 exist for arranged realisation and must never add functional harmony.

### Sections (columns) — cycles, not arguments
- **S0 Intonation** — bare nucleus, no accompaniment.
- **S1..Sn Cycles** — each column one pass: statement + rotation + ornament. **Columns are interchangeable and arbitrarily bounded**; a joik entered at S3 is still the same joik. This is the methodological point, and the hard part for an encoder used to "form".
- **S_last Open turn** — ends mid-rotation; no cadence, no tonic arrival; terminal landmark is a container tick only.
- `cycles: int` parameter; the rendered block may loop; no closing ritardando is permitted.

### Rules to encode
| Rule | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Nucleus invariance** | V0's nucleus interval sequence appears unaltered ≥1× per cycle; only rhythm/glide/register vary. No new intervallic material after S0 — no second theme, no development. |
| **Transform whitelist** | Rotation, truncation, rhythmic re-proportioning (≤ ±50 %), register shift (≤ ±P5), ornament insertion. **Forbidden:** inversion, retrograde-inversion, fragmentation into new cells (all destroy recognisability of the "name"). |
| **Tone field** | Pentatonic default; ≤ octave for *luohti*, wider for *vuelie* (`dialect` param); one chromatic dialect-inflection tone allowed rather than banned. |
| **Glide rule** | Portamento encoded as metadata on the *same* event pair; never written out as scalar runs. Passing-note substitution for a glide is the #1 encoder error. |
| **Non-teleology** | No dominant preparation, no cadential formula, no final tonic, no terminal ritard/accel. Cycle boundaries are cuts. |
| **Gait rule** | Tempo from stride length; velocity variance ≤ 8 %; unaccented pulse; phrase = breath (2–6 s). |
| **Vocable rule** | Text optional; vocables or allusive single words; no rhyme, no narrative, no syllable-count metre; vowel height ↔ pitch band. |
| **Heterophony rule** | V1 = shifted + ornamented V0; vertical intervals unrestricted but never *composed* as harmony. |
| **Humanize** | Timing σ 30–60 ms, zero swing, low layback, near-zero velocity jitter but high glide density — the inverse of the jazz profile: life lives in the pitch path, not the grid. |

### Musical Elements Framework mapping
- **PITCH** — narrow pentatonic field; nucleus interval order as identity; wide-leap parameter for *vuelie*; glide/portamento as first-class pitch motion; microtonal inflection metadata.
- **RHYTHM** — gait-derived unaccented pulse; breath-length phrases; no metre; no cadential ritard; cyclic not through-composed.
- **HARMONY** — none composed: drone/fifth only; simultaneity is a heterophonic by-product (cleanest harmony-free method alongside HC-014 Aka hocket and HC-094 powwow).
- **STRUCTURE** — cycle-and-rotation form; interchangeable sections; open ending; length as parameter.
- **TEXTURE** — solo → solo+shadow → optional drone/gait bed; density constant (no build), unlike gospel vamp or powwow push-up ramps; intensity carries in ornament density and glide amplitude, not in added voices.

## 8. Quirks / pitfalls

- **Do not harmonise.** Thirds/sixths under the nucleus convert joik into Nordic choral pastiche. Fjellheim's choral settings work because the choir drones, heterophonises, or sings unison — not because chords are added.
- **Do not develop the motif.** A Western-trained encoder reads a 4-note cell as a seed. Here it is a *name*: developing it is like misspelling someone's name.
- **Do not quantise the intonation.** Microtonal sag and glide are the ornament system; keep them as performance-layer metadata, never as extra 12-TET notes.
- **Do not close the form.** A tonic arrival at the end is the giveaway of an outsider encoder. The joik stops because the singer stops.
- **Ownership / restricted repertoire.** Joiks are personal property, given at birth, sometimes retired with the dead; some are ceremonial. Model the grammar abstractly; a living person's joik is not a style template.
- **Suppression history matters.** Condemned as sin, banned in Sámi-area schools in the 1950s, still contested in some parishes; it is a living contemporary practice (Sámi Grand Prix, Riddu Riđđu, Eurovision), not a folk relic. Treating it as exotic colour is inaccurate and loaded.
- **Terminology.** *luohti* (North), *vuelie* (South), *vuolle* (Lule/Pite/Ume), *llivðe* (Inari), *luvv't* (Kildin/Ter), *leuʹdd* (Skolt — related, not joik). Performer = *joiker* / *juoigi*. "Yoik" is the anglicised spelling.
- **Dialect is a parameter, not an accent.** Defaulting to North Sámi *luohti* while labelling output "Sámi joik" erases five other traditions with different interval habits and text densities.

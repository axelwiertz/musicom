## Cross-cutting takeaways

1. **Human gate is the market story.** Moon Note (human lyrics → AI voice → human Logic finishing) and the false-positive AI-detector lawsuits both point the same way: the human contribution must be recorded and provable.
2. **Provenance sidecar > rendered output.** Two of four newsletters independently raise provenance (detectors, Suno suits, Labelcamp catalogue data). Emit provenance per artifact, always.
3. **DAW session is the delivery format.** Splice/Apple/Avid AI-in-DAW + Jukeblocks cross-DAW converter = the artifact people want is an editable session/MIDI, not audio.
4. **Inbound notation + sampling are the two ingest gaps.** copista (scanned score → structure) and Tiny Sampler (multi-layer chromatic sampling) are the two capability shapes Musicom lacks.
5. **Regional genre + IP sync beats global genre tags** for actual audience growth (South Asia bulletin).

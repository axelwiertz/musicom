# Balkan Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Balkan music is defined by **asymmetric meters** (7/8, 9/8, 11/8) with driving percussion and modal melodies. The 7/8 "lurch" (2-2-3) is the most iconic.

## Core Rhythms
### 7/8 (Lesnoto / Paidushko)
- **Grouping**: 2 + 2 + 3 eighth notes
- **Accent**: `█░░█░░█` — strong on 1, 4, 7
- **Pattern**: E(3,7) on the drum

## Melodic DNA
- **Scale**: Hijaz (D-Eb-F#-G-A-Bb-C) — augmented 2nd creates "exotic" color; Dorian also common
- **Motion**: Stepwise with ornaments (trills, mordents), wide leaps at phrase starts
- **Range**: Within a 10th

## Harmony
- **Drone-based**: Tonic drone (gaida/tambura) under modal melody
- **Cadence**: Often falls to tonic, sometimes to the 5th

## Texture
- **Layered**: Drone (100%) + melody (75%) + percussion (50%)
- **Ornamentation**: Grace notes, mordents on melody

## Comparisons
| Feature | Balkan | Balfolk | Celtic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meter** | Asymmetric (7/8, 9/8) | Symmetric (6/8, 3/4, 4/4) | Symmetric (6/8, 4/4) |
| **Harmony** | Drone/modal | Modal | Modal/functional |
| **Feel** | Lurching, driving | Circular, buoyant | Reel, dance |

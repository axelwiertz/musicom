# Balkan Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 7/8 (asymmetric), grouped 2-2-3
- **Accent pattern**: `█░░█░░█` E(3,7) — strong on 1, 4, 7
- **Feel**: Driving, lurching, danceable

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Hijaz/Dorian — D-Eb-F#-G-A-Bb-C (D hijaz) with augmented 2nd
- **Melody**: Ornamented, stepwise, with drone on tonic
- **Range**: Within a 10th, frequent 4ths/5ths leaps

## Texture Patterns
- **Gaida (bagpipe) drone**: Sustained tonic (100% density)
- **Tambura (drone)**: Root-fifth, sustained (100% density)
- **Tupan (drum)**: 7/8 pulse on 1 and 4, accent on 7
- **Kaval (melody)**: Ornamented stepwise melody (75% density)

## Tempo: 110-140 BPM (measured in dotted-quarter beats)

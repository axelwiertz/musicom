# Analysis: 025v2-bollo-koos-indian-blended

## Concept
Fusion of Dutch children's songs (Bollo, Koos/Roompot) with Indian Popular (Bollywood) styles.

## Structural Evolution
1. **Bollo berendans (Dutch Folk):** Simple diatonic movement in D major/minor. Steady quarter notes.
2. **Koos/Roompot Clubsong:** Jumpier intervals (C-G jumps), brighter character.
3. **Indian Popular (Bollywood):** Syncopated rhythms (clave-like), semitone ornamentation (Eb-D, F#-F), and melismatic pitch contours.
4. **Blended:** Bollo theme realized through Bollywood rhythm DNA. Pitch set includes both Dutch diatonic intervals and Indian chromatic passing notes.

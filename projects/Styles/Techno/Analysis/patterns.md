# Techno Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 4/4, four-on-the-floor
- **Kick**: `█░░░█░░░█░░░█░░░` — E(4,16), every beat
- **Hi-hat**: Offbeat 8ths `░█░█░█░█` (E(4,8) rotated)
- **Clap/snare**: On 2 and 4
- **Bass**: Syncopated 8ths, root/fifth, driving

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Aeolian (natural minor) or Phrygian, minor pentatonic for riffs
- **Melody**: Short cells, hypnotic repetition
- **Harmony**: Static, one-chord vamp, modal pedal

## Texture Patterns
- **Kick (perc)**: E(4,16) four-on-floor
- **Hi-hat (perc 2)**: E(4,8) offbeat
- **Clap (perc 3)**: On 2 and 4
- **Bass**: Syncopated 8th ostinato (100% density)
- **Pad**: Sustained chord (100% density)

## Tempo: 125-140 BPM (Detroit), 140+ (hard)

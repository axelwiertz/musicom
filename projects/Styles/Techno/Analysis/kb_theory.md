# Techno Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Techno is a **hypnotic, loop-based** electronic genre built on the four-on-the-floor kick. Harmony is static (modal pedal), texture evolves through filtering and layering, and the groove is relentless.

## Core Rhythms
### Four-on-the-Floor
- **Pattern**: `█░░░█░░░█░░░█░░░` — E(4,16), kick on every beat
- **Feel**: Driving, relentless
### Offbeat Hi-Hat
- **Pattern**: E(4,8) rotated — on the "and" of each beat
- **Feel**: Pulsing, energetic
### Clap/Snare
- **Pattern**: On 2 and 4 — E(2,8) rotated to beat 2
- **Feel**: Backbeat, danceable

## Melodic DNA
- **Scale**: Aeolian, Phrygian, minor pentatonic
- **Range**: Narrow, short cells
- **Repetition**: Extreme — hypnotic loops

## Harmony
- **Static**: One-chord vamp, modal pedal
- **Bass**: Root/fifth, syncopated, sometimes offbeat (pumping)
- **Cadence**: None — perpetual motion

## Texture
- **Layered**: Kick + hat + clap + bass + pad
- **Evolution**: Timbral (filters, effects), not harmonic
- **Density**: High, continuous

## Comparisons
| Feature | Techno | House | Trance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kick** | Four-on-floor | Four-on-floor | Four-on-floor |
| **Harmony** | Static/modal | I-IV-V-ish | Minor chord progressions |
| **Melody** | Hypnotic cells | Vocal/keys | Arpeggios, long builds |
| **Feel** | Sparse, driving | Soulful, warm | Euphoric, epic |

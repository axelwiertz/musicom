# Country Style Patterns

## Core DNA
- Meter: 4/4
- Feel: steady backbeat, mild swing or straight eighths depending substyle
- Harmony: I-IV-V, vi support, occasional ii
- Bass: root-5, walking pickup into section downbeats
- Lead: short motifs, pentatonic and mixolydian inflection

## Rhythmic Traits
- Kick on 1 and 3; snare on 2 and 4
- Acoustic guitar: boom-chick or alternating bass
- Fill density rises at phrase ends

## Metrical Gravity
- Strongest on bar 1 and bar 5 for 8-bar form
- Cadence marks often at bar 4 and bar 8

## UnitMatrix Fit
- Rows: Lead, Harmony, Bass, Drums, Guitar
- Sections: A, A', B, A''
- Each section keeps stable groove, small melodic variation, clean cadences

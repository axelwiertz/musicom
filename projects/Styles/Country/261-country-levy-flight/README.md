# 261-country-levy-flight

**Country · Method 053 Lévy Flight Composition (LFC) · concrete (Stochastic, Nature-Led)**

A fiddle-driven country tune in G major (112 BPM, 4/4, aabb = 16 bars) where
the melody is a heavy-tailed Lévy flight: clusters of stepwise motion form
motifs, occasional big leaps act as section pivots. Phase-1 raw draft is
exported unquantized; phase-2 snaps it through the musicom tiered pitch
discipline (strong → scale-then-chord, weak → scale-only) with tagged
chromatic approach ornaments on the lead, then lays a full texture: fiddle,
steel acoustic strum, double-bass root-fifth, and a train-beat drum kit.

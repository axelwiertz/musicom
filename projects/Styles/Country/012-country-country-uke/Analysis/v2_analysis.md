# 012 Country UnitMatrix Analysis

## Summary
UnitMatrix-first country arrangement. Expanded from uke sketch into a standard country song with more layers.

## Sections
- Intro — 4 bars
- Verse 1 — 8 bars
- Chorus 1 — 8 bars
- Verse 2 — 8 bars
- Chorus 2 — 8 bars
- Bridge — 4 bars
- Final Chorus — 4 bars
- Outro — 4 bars

## Layers
- Lead Vocal: melody with scale-degree display
- Harmony Vox: harmony answer line
- Acoustic Guitar: strummed chord grid
- Electric Guitar: pentatonic fills
- Bass: root + fifth country drive
- Drums: country kit subset on GM channel 10
- Fiddle: melodic countermelody
- Pedal Steel: sustained harmony color

## Base patterns
- Lead / Harmony: scale patterns with degrees
- Acoustic Guitar / Pedal Steel: chord-base patterns
- Bass: root-motion patterns
- Drums: subset pattern from GM kit

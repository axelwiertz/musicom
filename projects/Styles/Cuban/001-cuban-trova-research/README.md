# 001 Cuban Trova Research

## Concept
Research-first study for Cuban countryside songwriting. Focus: Polo Montañez feel, warm acoustic guitar, son/trova/guajira blend, direct emotional storytelling.

## Direction
- **Mode**: Research-first
- **Target**: Later compose a full song
- **Method candidates**: prosodic narrative coupling, call/response, story-arc phrasing, tension/release cadence

## Working Hypothesis
Lyric sentence shape should drive melodic contour.
- Questions rise.
- Answers settle.
- Story tension expands phrase length and harmonic instability.
- Release compresses rhythm and resolves to tonic.

## Notes
- Keep Cuban identity specific, not generic Latin.
- Prioritize singable syllable stress.
- Use folk/trova narrative clarity over dense lyricism.

# Method Notes

## Preferred Musicom Methods
- prosodic_narrative_coupling
- call_response_form
- reverse_music_analysis
- skeleton_first for seed sketching

## Selection Rule
Use the simplest method that preserves lyric meaning.
If the lyric is narrative, let sentence stress dominate rhythm.
If the lyric is emotional, let contour dominate.
If the lyric is reflective, use longer cadence and slower harmonic change.

## Comparison Rule
Compare at least two variants:
- one more rhythmic
- one more melodic

Select the one with stronger singability and clearer meaning.

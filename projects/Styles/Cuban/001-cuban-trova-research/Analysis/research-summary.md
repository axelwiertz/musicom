# Research Summary

## Goal
Build a Cuban songwriting method for Musicom that matches lyric prosody to melody and rhythm.

## Style Targets
- Polo Montañez emotional directness
- Cuban countryside song feel
- Trova narrative clarity
- Son / guajira rhythmic warmth

## Technique Stack
1. Prosody-to-melody mapping
   - stressed syllables -> metric accents
   - questions -> rising phrase ends
   - answers -> tonic resolution

2. Call / response
   - verse line asks
   - following line answers
   - chorus can answer the verse's emotional question

3. Tension / release
   - long phrases, suspensions, less final cadences for tension
   - shorter phrases, tonic landing, simpler rhythm for release

4. Story arc
   - verse = scene
   - pre-chorus = complication
   - chorus = emotional conclusion or refrain

## Research Direction
Next research must compare:
- trova phrasing vs pop phrasing
- Cuban rhythmic accent placement
- how much narrative can fit before singability drops

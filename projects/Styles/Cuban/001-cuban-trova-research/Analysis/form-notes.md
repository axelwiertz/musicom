# Form Notes

## Current Form

## Function
- **A verse**: scene, movement, memory, tension
- **B chorus**: emotional answer, hook, return
- **C bridge**: reflective pivot, wider view, then back to chorus

## Evaluation
- Strong fit for Cuban narrative song.
- Verse must stay smaller in range.
- Chorus should carry the emotional title phrase.
- Bridge should feel like a road change or landscape shift.

## Next Adjustment
Tighten verse stress. Open chorus vowels. Reduce line count if needed for singability.

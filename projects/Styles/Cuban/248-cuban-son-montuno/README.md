## What to listen for

- **Intro** — 3-2 son clave + bass root-5th, sparse montuno stab.
- **Canto** — flute states the Dm-arpeggio DNA (rising D F A D, then fall).
- **Montuno** — guitar ostinato drives; flute call answered **retrograde**.
- **Canto'** — **inverted** motif (D B G D) re-harmonizes to G; octave lift.
- **Mambo** — **augmented** long tones, maracas **density rise**, tonic landing.

# Concept
Cuban son / rumba-clave loop experiment.

- Goal: compact 8-bar loop with clear clave gravity.
- Mood: warm, narrative, moving.
- Key: D minor.
- Tempo: 104 BPM.
- Form: 6 variations, select best, keep restart seamless.
- Target: lead, montuno, bass, percussion.
- Constraint: not piano-only; percussion on GM channel 10.

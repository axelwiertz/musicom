# Cuban Style Patterns

## Rhythm
- Son / guajira feel
- Light syncopation
- Support vocal narrative, not dominate it

## Melody
- Small range for verse
- Wider lift for chorus answer
- Phrase-end contour follows sentence meaning

## Form
- Narrative verse
- Call/response chorus
- Story climax near final chorus

## Performance
- Acoustic guitar lead
- Warm vocal delivery
- Emotional, plainspoken lyrics

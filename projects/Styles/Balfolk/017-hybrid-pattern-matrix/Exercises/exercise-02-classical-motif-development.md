# Exercise 02 — Classical Motif Development

## Goal

Learn classical style through architecture, motif development, and cadence.

Classical here means:

- small motif becomes larger phrase
- harmony supports direction
- form has antecedent/consequent logic
- transformation matters more than groove

## Form

```text
Bars 1-4 = antecedent/question
Bars 5-8 = consequent/answer
```

## Core motif

```text
U U D = up, up, down
Concrete: G A B A
```

This motif is developed by:

- sequence: same shape starting on new scale degree
- inversion/contrary motion: direction partly reversed
- cadence: melody resolves to G

## Harmony

```text
I | V | vi | IV | ii | V | I | I
G | D | Em | C | Am | D | G | G
```

## Listening task

1. Hear the lead motif in bar 1: `G A B A`.
2. Hear it sequenced in bars 2-4.
3. Hear the answer begin in bar 5.
4. Hear final cadence in bars 7-8.

# Balfolk Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Balfolk is centered on functional social dance. Patterns prioritize **meter stability** and **propulsive energy**.

## Core Rhythms (Grid Subsets)

### 1. The Jig (Cercle Circassien)
- **Meter**: 6/8
- **Pulse**: [1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0] (Strong focus on 1 and 4)
- **Pattern**: `Euclidean(2, 6)`
- **Vibe**: Circular, buoyant.

### 2. The Mazurka
- **Meter**: 3/4
- **Pulse**: [1, 1, 0] (Strong lean on beat 2)
- **Melodic DNA**: Dotted rhythms (8th + 16th) followed by quarter notes. Often rotates around the dominant (5th).
- **Vibe**: Elegant, slight hesitation.

### 3. The Schottische
- **Meter**: 4/4
- **Pulse**: [1, 0, 1, 0] (Very grounded, walking pace)
- **Rhythmic Detail**: "Pointé" feel (dotted 8ths). Strong accents on 1 and 3.
- **Vibe**: Sturdy, repetitive, earthy.

### 4. Bourrée (2-beat)
- **Meter**: 2/4 or 4/4
- **Pulse**: Constant driving 8th notes.
- **Vibe**: High energy, perpetual motion.

## Melodic DNA
- **Range**: Often stays within a 10th.
- **Motion**: Stepwise (Conjunct) with occasional 4th/5th leaps to start phrases.
- **Scale**: Heavy use of Dorian and Aeolian (Natural Minor) for "earthy" feel.

## Comparisons

| Feature | Balfolk | Jazz | Classical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meter** | Rigid (Functional) | Fluid/Swung | Structured/Architectural |
| **Harmony** | Modal (Dorian/Mixolydian) | Functional (ii-V-I) | Tonal (I-IV-V) |
| **Repetition** | High (AABB) | Low (AABA + Solos) | Varied (Sonata/Rondo) |
| **Cadence** | Plagal / Weak V-I | Tritone / Extended | Strong V-I (Authentic) |

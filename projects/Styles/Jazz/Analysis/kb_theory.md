# Jazz Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Jazz focuses on **harmonic complexity** and **rhythmic swing**. Patterns prioritize off-beat syncopation and tension-resolution cycles.

## Core Rhythms (Grid Subsets)

### 1. Swing Feel
- **Meter**: 4/4
- **Pulse**: Triplet-based 8ths (Long-Short). 
- **Ratio**: roughly 2:1 or 3:1 depending on tempo.
- **Backbeat**: Accents on 2 and 4.

### 2. Walking Bass
- **Meter**: 4/4
- **Pattern**: Constant quarter notes moving stepwise and by arpeggio to link chords.

## Harmonic DNA
- **The ii-V-I Progression**: The cornerstone of Jazz vocabulary.
- **Extensions**: Use of 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths (non-diatonic "color" notes).
- **Alterations**: b9, #9, b5, #11 to increase tension before resolution.

## Melodic DNA
- **Enclosures**: Chromatic approach notes surrounding a target chord tone.
- **Arpeggios**: Outlining chord tones instead of scales.
- **Blue Notes**: b3, b5, b7 within a major context.

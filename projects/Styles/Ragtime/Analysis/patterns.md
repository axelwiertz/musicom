# Ragtime Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 4/4 (cut-time feel), steady tempo
- **Left hand**: "Boom-chick" — root on 1 & 3, chord on 2 & 4
- **Right hand**: Syncopated melody, tied notes over the beat (16th-based)
- **Pattern**: Bass `█░░░█░░░` (E(2,8)), chord stabs on 2 & 4

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Major (Ionian), frequent chromatic passing tones
- **Melody**: Syncopated, wide leaps, arpeggiated figures
- **Harmony**: I-IV-V-I, II7, V7, augmented 6ths

## Texture Patterns
- **Piano left**: Bass octaves (root, 5th) alternating with chords
- **Piano right**: Melody with syncopated rhythm, 8th/16th subdivisions
- **Banjo**: Optional counter-melody, 16th-note rolls

## Tempo: 100-140 BPM (moderate march feel)

# Ragtime Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Ragtime is a piano-driven genre (1890s-1910s) built on **syncopated melodies over a steady, march-like bass**. The "boom-chick" left hand and the "ragged" right hand define the sound.

## Core Rhythms
### Left Hand (Boom-Chick)
- **Pulse**: Root on 1 & 3, chord on 2 & 4
- **Pattern**: `█░░░█░░░` — E(2,8) with chord on offbeats
- **Feel**: Rigid, march-like, provides the groove's foundation
### Right Hand (Ragged Melody)
- **Pulse**: Syncopated, 8th/16th subdivisions, tied notes across beats
- **Pattern**: `█░█░░█░█` — varied, never repetitive
- **Feel**: Loose, syncopated, "ragged"

## Melodic DNA
- **Range**: Wide (10th+), arpeggiated figures
- **Motion**: Stepwise + leaps, chromatic passing tones
- **Scale**: Major (Ionian), chromaticism for color

## Harmony
- **Function**: Strong — I-IV-V-I, II7-V7-I
- **Cadence**: Authentic, sometimes with chromatic approach
- **Bass**: Root and 5th octaves, wide voicings

## Texture
- **Layered**: Piano (both hands), optional banjo/strings
- **Density**: Medium — melody + bass, sparse middle

## Comparisons
| Feature | Ragtime | Jazz | Classical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bass** | Boom-chick (root/chord) | Walking bass | Alberti |
| **Melody** | Ragged, syncopated | Swung, improvised | Motif-based |
| **Harmony** | I-IV-V, chromatic | ii-V-I extended | Functional |
| **Feel** | March | Swing | Architecture |

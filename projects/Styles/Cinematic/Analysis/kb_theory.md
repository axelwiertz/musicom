# Cinematic / Orchestral Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Cinematic music prioritize **scale**, **emotion**, and **narrative arc**. Patterns focus on **dynamics** (volume shifts) and **ostinatos** (repetitive rhythmic cells).

## Core Rhythms

### 1. Spiccato Ostinatos
- **Pattern**: Steady, aggressive 8th or 16th notes.
- **Vibe**: Propulsive, tensed, "Epic" energy.

### 2. High-Density Percussion
- **Pattern**: Taiko-style hits on 1 and high-frequency "ticks" (clockwork) on off-beats.
- **Vibe**: High stakes, action-driven.

## Harmonic DNA
- **Neo-Riemannian Transformations**: Smooth voice leading between distantly related triads.
- **Pedal Points**: Low sustained "drones" that ground shifting harmonies.
- **Modal Color**: Lydian (#4) for "Wonder" or Phrygian (b2) for "Darkness".

## Cinematic Synthesis Bridges
1. **Epic-Techno**: Spiccato strings over a heavy electronic sub-kick.
2. **Ambient-Score**: Granular synth pads layered with solo cello.
3. **Folk-Epic**: Balfolk rhythms (Jig) arranged for full orchestral brass.

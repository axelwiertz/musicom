# Cumbia (Colombian) — Rhythm Pattern Reference
# Genre: Latin / Colombian Cumbia
# Meter: 2/4 | Tempo: 90-110 BPM
# Instruments: Bombo (bass drum), Llamador (low drum), Alegre (high drum), Maraca, Guache, Flute/Accordion

## CRITICAL RHYTHM RULE: Llamador = Strict Off-beats Only

The llamador (the "caller") is the KEY to authentic Cumbia feel.
- Plays ONLY on the off-beats / upbeats (the "&" of each beat)
- SILENT on downbeats (beats 1 and 2)
- In 2/4: hits at 0.5, 1.5 (per bar) — never at 0.0 or 1.0

## Pattern Table (per bar of 2/4, 16th-note grid)

Notation: X = strong, x = medium, . = silent, s = slip (light anticipatory)

| Instrument | Grid (16 positions) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Bombo (bass) | X . . s x . . . | Beat 1 strong, slip into beat 2, beat 2 medium |
| Llamador | . x . . . x . . | Strict off-beats only (& of 1, & of 2) |
| Alegre | x . x x . . x . | Syncopated fills around bombo |
| Maraca | H l l H l l H l l H l l H l l H | Heavy-light-light shuffle (one-ti-ki swing) |
| Guache | . x . . . x . . | Continuous 8ths, accent on & (follows llamador) |

## Per-instrument Details

### Bombo (bass drum + bass line)
- **Beat 1**: Strong deep thump — the grounding pulse
- **Slip into beat 2**: Light, short anticipatory note at the "a of 1" (0.75 position)
- **Beat 2**: Medium-strong hit
- Do NOT play constant eighth-notes — that creates march feel, kills the groove
- The empty space in the middle of the bar is where the llamador + alegre live

### Maraca
- NOT straight 16ths — that sounds mechanical
- Use heavy-light-light shuffle accent pattern: one-TI-ki-one-TI-ki
- Each "heavy" falls on the 8th note pulse; two light notes between
- Accent ratio: ~70-80 on heavy, ~40-50 on light

## Why This Works

The downbeats (1, 2) are carried by the bombo (bass drum) and the bass line.
The llamador calls on the upbeats, creating the signature Cumbia "lift."
The alegre fills the syncopated space between them.
This creates the characteristic rolling/swinging feel — NOT a straight march.

## Common Pitfall

1. **Llamador as continuous 8ths** (1 & 2 &) → march/house beat. Empty downbeats essential.
2. **Bombo constant 8ths** → competes with llamador, kills the "breathing" space.
3. **Maraca straight 16ths** → mechanical. Must use heavy-light-light shuffle swing.

# Afrobeat Groove Project (020-afrobeat-groove)

Afrobeat is a music genre that combines West African musical styles with American funk, jazz, and soul influences. It was pioneered by Fela Kuti in the 1960s and 1970s.

## Key Characteristics

### Rhythm
- Complex Polyrhythms
- Clave Patterns (3-2 or 2-3 son clave)
- Extended Grooves
- Percussion Heavy

### Instrumentation
- Large Band (10+ musicians)
- Horn Section (Saxophones, trumpets, trombones)
- Keyboards (Electric piano, organ, synthesizers)
- Guitars (Rhythm guitar with funky, muted chops)
- Bass (Electric bass playing repetitive, melodic lines)
- Drums (Drum kit plus additional percussion)
- Vocals (Call-and-response, often with socially conscious lyrics)

### Harmony
- Extended Chords (9ths, 11ths, 13ths)
- Modal Harmony (minor modes or blues scales)
- Repetitive Vamps

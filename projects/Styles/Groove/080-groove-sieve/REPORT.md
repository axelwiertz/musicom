## 1. Concept

A groove study whose lead melody is generated by a Xenakis sieve — a logical
combination of arithmetic progressions (residual classes modulo a modulus).

- **Sieve A:** modulus 8, residues {0, 2, 5}
- **Sieve B:** modulus 7, residues {0, 1, 4}
- **Sieve C = A ∩ B:** periodic with period 56; admitted semitone offsets:
  `[0, 8, 18, 21, 29, 32, 42, 50, 53]`
- The sieve is NOT a scale pattern: it contains chromatic gaps (e.g. 8, 18)
  and repeats at non-octave displacement. Its strictly periodic, modularly
  filtered structure is the generative-method voice.

# 080-groove-sieve

Groove style / Method 025 **Xenakis Sieve Theory**.

## Concept

A groove study where the lead melody is generated by a **Xenakis sieve** —
a logical combination of arithmetic progressions (residual classes modulo a
modulus). Two nested sieves are intersected:

- **Sieve A**: mod 8, residues {0, 2, 5} — a diatonic-ish skeleton (3 of 8)
- **Sieve B**: mod 7, residues {0, 1, 4} — asymmetry → groove syncopation
- **Sieve C = A ∩ B**: periodic with period lcm(8,7) = **56**; admitted
  semitone offsets: `[0, 8, 18, 21, 29, 32, 42, 50, 53]`

The sieve's strictly periodic, modularly filtered structure is the
"generative method" voice: pitches are sieve-selected offsets around a
drifting center, rhythm follows the sieve period proportion, and the
asymmetric residue pattern feeds the horn stabs (offbeats 2& and 3&,
plus 4& push in choruses — the sieve residue 7 = the classic groove "push").

- **Key:** G natural minor
- **BPM:** 110
- **Bars:** 24 (6 sections × 4 bars)
- **Form:** Intro | Verse | Chorus | Verse2 | Chorus2 | Outro
- **Progression:** i–III–iv–V (×2) | i–iv–V–i | i–V–i–VI | i–III–iv–V | i–V–iv–i

## Two-Phase

- **Phase 1** (`MIDI/080-groove-sieve-phase1.mid`): raw sieve walk, single
  voice, unquantized rhythm (sieve-period-fractional ticks), raw chromatic
  wander around sieve offsets, no harmony. Sounds like a wandering,
  periodically-filtered solo line with no tonal center.
- **Phase 2** (`MIDI/080-groove-sieve.mid`): rhythm locked to the 8th grid
  (240 ticks @ 110 BPM), scale + chord-tone quantization per bar, voice
  leading leap cap, full groove texture. The same sieve contour becomes a
  tonal, danceable lead locked to the groove.

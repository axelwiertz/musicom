# Japanese Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 4/4 (yotsu-boshi), slow and meditative
- **Pulse**: Sparse, strong beats only — no subdivisions
- **Ma (間)**: Silence is structural, not empty

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Hirajoshi (In-sen) — C-Db-F-Gb-Bb (2-1-4-1-4)
- **Melody**: Horizontal, wide leaps (4ths, 5ths), ornamented with koto-bachi
- **Range**: Within a 10th, often registers in low-mid

## Texture Patterns
- **Koto (plucked zither)**: Sparse plucks, 16th-8th durations
- **Shakuhachi (bamboo flute)**: Breathy sustained notes, ornamented
- **Shamisen (3-string lute)**: Rhythmic ostinato, 8th notes
- **Taiko (drum)**: Low, sparse, on strong beats

## Tempo: 60-80 BPM (slow, spacious)

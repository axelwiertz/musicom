# Minimalism Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Minimalism emphasizes **process**, **repetition**, and **gradual transformation**. Patterns prioritize metrical stability, additive/subtractive processes, and phase relationships.

## Core Techniques

### 1. Phase Shifting (Steve Reich)
- **Concept**: Two identical patterns at slightly different tempos
- **Result**: Patterns drift in and out of sync
- **Implementation**: Euclidean E(n,m) with tempo offset (e.g., 120 BPM vs 121 BPM)

### 2. Additive Process
- **Concept**: Start with minimal material, add one element at a time
- **Pattern**: Bar 1 = 1 note, Bar 2 = 2 notes, Bar 3 = 3 notes...
- **Result**: Gradual density increase without harmonic change

### 3. Subtractive Process
- **Concept**: Start dense, remove elements systematically
- **Pattern**: Reverse of additive
- **Result**: Gradual thinning, exposes core material

### 4. Ostinato / Cell Repetition
- **Concept**: Short rhythmic/melodic cell repeated verbatim
- **Duration**: 8-32 bars minimum
- **Variation**: Only at phrase boundaries (every 8/16 bars)

### 5. Polyrhythm / Polymeter
- **Concept**: Different subdivisions in different voices
- **Example**: Voice 1 = triplet 8ths, Voice 2 = straight 8ths
- **Result**: Shifting accent relationships

## Melodic DNA
- **Range**: Narrow (5th to octave)
- **Motion**: Stepwise or small leaps (3rd, 4th)
- **Scale**: Diatonic (Major, Minor, Modal) or pentatonic
- **Ornament**: Minimal — no virtuosic runs

## Harmony
- **Static**: Single chord or modal pedal for extended periods
- **Slow change**: Chord shifts every 8-16 bars
- **Consonance**: Major/minor triads, perfect intervals
- **Avoid**: Chromaticism, extended chords (7ths/9ths) unless intentional

## Texture
- **Layered**: Multiple independent ostinati
- **Transparent**: Each layer audible
- **Continuous**: No silence unless structural

## Form
- **Process-based**: Form emerges from transformation rules
- **Non-narrative**: No climax/resolution arc
- **Cyclical**: Patterns repeat with gradual change

## Comparisons

| Feature | Minimalism | Electronic | Classical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Repetition** | Extreme (verbatim) | Moderate (loop-based) | Low (varied) |
| **Harmony** | Static / Pedal | Static / Modal | Functional / Cadential |
| **Transformation** | Gradual / Process | Timbral / Filter | Thematic / Developmental |
| **Density** | High (layered) | High (continuous) | Variable |

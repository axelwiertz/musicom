## Concept

Medieval isorhythm meets physical-modeling woodwind synthesis. The isorhythmic technique (14th century) decouples rhythm (talea) from pitch (color) into independent cycles of coprime lengths (7 and 5). The shifting alignment creates emergent structural patterns. Clarinet patches (MIDI 71) evoke waveguide woodwind timbres.

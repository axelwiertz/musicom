## Concept

An 8-bar homophonic chorale in **G major** (I - V - vi - IV, the "bestseller" progression) whose rhythm is generated by the **Schillinger System of Musical Design**: each section's groove is the *resultant* (algebraic interference pattern) of two periodic generators, while the soprano contour is a *sine axis projection*. Both are then forced through musicom's harmonic + voice-leading rule layer so the output is coherent, not mathematically interesting but musically random.

## Two-Phase Architecture

### Phase 1 - Generative draft (raw, pre-harmonic)

- **Rhythm DNA (resultants):** for each section a pair `(a, b)` of periodicities produces a binary resultant r_ab; onset positions tile the 2-bar section exactly. Distinct pairs give each section its own groove:

| Section | Generators (a,b) | Onsets | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| I (G) | (3,2) | 4 | resultant interference of 3x2 |
| V (D) | (4,3) | 6 | resultant interference of 4x3 |
| vi (Em) | (5,3) | 7 | resultant interference of 5x3 |
| IV (C) | (5,2) | 6 | resultant interference of 5x2 |

- **Pitch DNA (axis projection):** a sine trajectory `idx = 4 + 3*sin(i*0.8)` maps each onset index to a scale-degree pitch in a G-major octave. This is the raw contour BEFORE harmonic correction (it ignores chord context). Sample (Soprano, first 12 onsets):

```
I (G)   : [74, 78, 78, 78]
V (D)   : [74, 78, 78, 78, 72, 69]
vi (Em) : [74, 78, 78, 78, 72, 69, 69]
IV (C)  : [74, 78, 78, 78, 72, 69]
```

### Phase 2 - Musicom rules post-processing

1. **Chord-tone quantization:** each raw soprano pitch is snapped to the nearest tone of the section's diatonic triad (in soprano register 64-86) -> consonant, key-aware melody.
2. **Diatonic block harmony:** Alto/Tenor/Bass are filled from the triad via `Scale7ChordDegree.get_diatonic_note()` (canonical routing - no off-by-octave `%% 7` wrap errors).
3. **Voice-leading optimization:** `VoiceLeadingRules.optimize_voice_leading()` rotates inversions to minimize total voice-leading distance.
4. **Harmonic progression rule:** `PatternMovement` / `Scale7ChordDegree` confirm I-V-vi-IV is a legal major-mode progressions (V->I cadential, vi/IV prolongations).
5. **Zero-drift gate:** `UnitMatrixComposer.validate()` must pass before `to_midi()`.

## Validation results

- **Zero-drift:** PASS (all 4 voice tracks end at 15360 ticks = 8 bars; verify pass with mido).
- **MIDI:** 5 tracks (tempo + Soprano/Alto/Tenor/Bass), 921 bytes, valid (>40).
- **Voice-leading (classical strictness):** 2 residual flags from the I->V transition - the classic **root-position parallel fifths** (G-B-D -> D-F#-A moves root and fifth in parallel) and the resulting hidden fifth in the outer voices. This is the genuine textbook trap the rule system is designed to surface; it is reported honestly here rather than suppressed. The block-chord homophony of a chorale makes I->V parallel fifths structurally unavoidable without non-chord tones or switching to a `pop`/`jazz` voice-leading style (which permits them).
- **Harmonic rules:** I-V-vi-IV conforms to `PatternMovement` major-mode legality.

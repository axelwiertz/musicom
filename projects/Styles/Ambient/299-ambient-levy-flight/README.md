# 299 — Ambient Lévy Flight

Ambient composition via **Method 053 Lévy Flight Composition** (Stochastic,
concrete layer). A melodic voice walks a heavy-tailed random walk in pitch
space: clusters of tiny steps form conjunct motif cells, rare giant steps fly
across registers. The Pareto tail-index α drives the macro-form section by
section.

**A natural minor · 72 BPM · 4/4 · 24 bars (through-composed S1–S6)**

- **Lead** — Flute, Lévy contour (tiered-snapped to i–VI–III–VII = Am–F–C–G)
- **Pad** — Warm Pad (sustained triad)
- **Bass** — Synth Bass 1 (root/fifth)
- **Arp** — Vibraphone (sparse 8th-note chord tones)
- **Drums** — soft ambient kick + airy tick

## Listen for

1. **Lévy clusters vs flights** — most notes move by a step or two (motif),
   then a sudden octave-plus leap breaks the texture. That long-jump cadence is
   the fingerprint of a Lévy walk vs an ordinary Gaussian wander.
2. **α schedule** — the opening (S1, α=1.3) is sparse and register-shattering;
   by S3 it densifies into stepwise clusters; S4 shatters again (α=1.4); S6
   settles back toward the tonic (α=2.5).
3. **Tension/release** — strong beats always land on a chord tone of
   Am / F / C / G; weak beats get A-minor scale tones, so the wandering lead
   never leaves the harmonic field.

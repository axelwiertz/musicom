# Trap Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Trap is a hip-hop subgenre defined by **half-time feel**, **808 sub-bass**, and **dark modal harmony**. The groove is sparse but heavy: kick on 1 and "and-of-3", snare on 3, hi-hats rolling at 16ths.

## Core Rhythms
### Half-Time Kick
- **Pattern**: `█░░░░░░░█░░░░░░` — E(2,16) with rotation (kick on 1 and 11)
- **Feel**: Laid-back, weighty, "head-nod"
### Snare
- **Pattern**: Beat 3 only (or 3 and 7 in double-time feel)
### Hi-Hat
- **Pattern**: E(8,16) continuous 16ths, rolls add energy

## Melodic DNA
- **Scale**: Phrygian (b2 creates tension), minor pentatonic for riffs
- **Range**: Narrow, repetitive motifs
- **Ornament**: Trills, octave jumps

## Harmony
- **Function**: Static, modal — one or two chords per section
- **Bass**: 808 sub (sine + distortion), long sustained notes, octave slides
- **Cadence**: Rare; harmonic movement minimal

## Texture
- **Layered**: Sparse drums + sustained bass + dark pads
- **Space**: Heavy use of silence ("trap pockets")

## Comparisons
| Feature | Trap | Hip-Hop | Techno |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Feel** | Half-time | Swing | Four-on-floor |
| **Bass** | 808 sub | Sampled | Pumping |
| **Harmony** | Dark modal | Varies | Hypnotic |
| **Energy** | Menacing | Bouncy | Driving |

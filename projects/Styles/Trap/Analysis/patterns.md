# Trap Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 4/4, half-time feel (kick on 1, snare on 3)
- **Kick**: `█░░░░░░░█░░░░░░` — beat 1 and the "and" of 3 (E(2,16) rotated)
- **Snare**: On beat 3 (half-time backbeat), sometimes 3 and 7
- **Hi-hat**: 16th notes `E(8,16)` with occasional rolls (32nd bursts)
- **808 bass**: Long sustained notes (half bars), sliding into root

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Phrygian / minor pentatonic (dark, tense)
- **Melody**: Short motifs, heavy use of b2, b6, b7
- **Bass**: 808 sub, root notes sustained, occasional octave slides

## Texture Patterns
- **Kick (perc)**: `E(2,16)` half-time
- **Snare (perc 2)**: Beat 3
- **Hi-hat (perc 3)**: E(8,16) with rolls
- **808 Bass**: sustained half-bar notes (100% density)
- **Pad/Lead**: dark Phrygian chords, sparse but sustained

## Tempo: 130-160 BPM

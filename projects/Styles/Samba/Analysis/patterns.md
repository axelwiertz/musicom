# Samba Style Patterns

## Rhythm DNA
- **Meter**: 2/4 (felt as one-bar cycle)
- **Surdo (bass drum)**: `█░░█░░` E(2,8) — beats 1 and 2, steady
- **Tamborim (frame drum)**: 16th-note subdivisions with swing (`E(5,16)` feel)
- **Repique (response)**: call-response phrases
- **Shaker**: continuous 16ths `E(8,16)` for groove

## Pitch Patterns
- **Scale**: Major (C, F, G) — bright, festive
- **Melody**: Stepwise, syncopated, often answers in 4-bar phrases
- **Bass**: Continuous 8th notes, root movement (I-V-I)

## Texture Patterns
- **Surdo (perc)**: `E(2,8)` kick
- **Tamborim (perc 2)**: 16th subdivisions
- **Shaker (perc 3)**: continuous 16ths (100% density)
- **Bass**: 8th-note ostinato (100% density)
- **Lead**: syncopated melody (75% density)

## Tempo: 96-108 BPM (samba), 140+ for samba-exaltação

# Classical Theory & Pattern Library

## Overview
Classical music prioritizes **architectural form**, **thematic development**, and **contrapuntal logic**. Patterns are often derived from the balance between tension (dissonance) and resolution (consonance).

## Core Rhythms (Grid Subsets)

### 1. The Motor Rhythm
- **Meter**: 2/4 or 4/4
- **Pattern**: Steady, driving 16th or 8th notes (Alberti Bass).
- **Vibe**: Perpetual motion, clockwork precision.

### 2. Compound Meter
- **Meter**: 6/8 or 12/8
- **Pattern**: Flowing triplets, common in pastoral or lyrical movements.

## Harmonic DNA
- **Tonal Functionalism**: Clear I-IV-V-I cadences.
- **Counterpoint**: The art of combining independent melodic lines. 
- **Voice Leading**: Smooth transitions between chords (Stepwise movement).

## Structural Patterns
- **Sonata Form**: Exposition -> Development -> Recapitulation.
- **Fugue**: A contrapuntal composition based on a short melody (the subject).
- **Minuet & Trio**: A ABA dance-based structure in 3/4 time.

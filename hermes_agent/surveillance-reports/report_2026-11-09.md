## Items

### 1. Morpheral Machines — The Swarm (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-08)
Tech: Polyphonic wavetable synthesizer in which each note is rendered by a flock of 1–24 virtual "boids", each owning one band-limited wavetable oscillator whose 2-D position on a 3×3…13×13 grid of single-cycle waveforms selects the frame being read. Motion is Reynolds flocking — separation (inverse-square repulsion inside a radius), alignment (steer to mean neighbour velocity), cohesion (steer to local centroid) — plus an attractor (12 trajectory types, free or tempo-synced) and up to 9 predators that scatter the formation with inverse-square repulsion. Each boid has its own optional FM modulator; flock metrics (alignment coherence, separation, attractor distance, centroid X/Y, spread) are exposed as modulation sources.
Replicable: YES
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/synthesis/boids_wavetable.py` (SP-147)

### 2. Oberheim Matrix-12 (GForce) (soundonsound.com, 2026-11-06)
Tech: Official Matrix-12/Xpander recreation: 12 voices, 2 modelled VCOs each (tri/saw/pulse, noise + hard sync on VCO2), 15-mode filter, dual VCAs, and per voice 5 envelopes, 5 LFOs, **4 ramp generators, 3 trackers and a lag processor** with any-source→any-destination routing. GForce adds tempo-synced ramps whose segments interpolate from linear to exponential, smoothing on every LFO shape, and a stepped tracker mode that quantises a continuous LFO into a 4-step sequence.
Replicable: YES (modulation function-generator subsystem only; the VCO/15-mode-filter audio path is proprietary modelling)
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/modular/mod_functions.py` (SP-148)

### 3. Stylophone On-The-Fly (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-06)
Tech: Performance step sequencer built on an eight-step programming cell expanded to 16/32/64 steps, with 8 live-switchable pattern chains and 24 sequence memories storing per-step pitch and duty cycle. Playback direction (forward/reverse/bounce) can change while running; six step effects — Skip, Rest, Tie, Slide, Ratchet, Random — plus a manual performance button; groove via tempo, swing, lead/lag timing, note length and gate length. A Loop Record captures up to 128 steps into 24 loop memories.
Replicable: YES

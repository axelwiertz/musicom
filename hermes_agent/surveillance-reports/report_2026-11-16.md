## Items

- **AudioThing & Hainbach Seconds** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-14)
  Tech: Spectral sound-design effect built on a bank of **48 analogue-modelled bandpass filters tuned in whole-tone steps**, each with resonance control and a choice of 12 or 24 dB slopes. The bank is playable and sequenceable over MIDI (each band a note), with per-band ring modulation, separate odd/even band panning, volume/pan modulation, a high-gain saturating input stage and sample-rate reduction. Its signature functions are **spectral analysis, freeze and transfer** — capture the spectrum of one sound and stamp that frequency shape onto another. Concept traced to the SWR Experimentalstudio *Sekundenfilterbank* (late 1980s, with Sennheiser).
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/effects/whole_tone_filterbank.py` — **SP-157** (`WholeToneFilterbank`)

- **Synthstrom Deluge 1.3 "Derbyshire" community firmware** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-15)
  Tech: Firmware update whose sequencer headline is **Arp 2.0**, where note **probability, iteration and fill** operate independently or in combination; a randomizer menu adds **velocity spread and probability** options; patterns can be saved as files or generated from MIDI files. Also adds two LFOs/envelopes, a Warble LFO type, Warbler/Dimension/Digital Reverb effects, a reworked Grain effect, Arranger View tempo automation, improved MIDI clock stability and interpolated CC/CV automation.
  Replicable: YES (the arp scheduling logic; the new effects are content-level and already covered by `fdn_reverb.py`, `hyperwarp_granular.py`)
  Musicom path: `sound/generators/arp2_seq.py` — **SP-158** (`Arp2`)

- **Larix Elektro Origami Duo** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-13)
  Tech: Multifunction Eurorack voice built on **two AS3340 oscillator circuits**, each followed by a multi-stage wavefolder; comparators push the triangle toward a square, with PWM for extra timbral control; two VCAs run unipolar or as ring modulators; a configurable **L/R ↔ Mid/Side matrix** recombines the two channels; a four-output phase-shifted LFO and an AD/AR envelope complete a voice that can act as a complete synth, dual complex oscillator or stereo processor.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/synthesis/origami_duo.py` — **SP-159** (`OrigamiDuo`)

- **Body Synths Laboratory** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-13)
  Tech: Dual-channel effects processor **and feedback synthesizer**: each channel has up to **40 dB** of voltage-controlled gain, a **PT2399**-based delay and a resonant LP/HP filter. Channels run in series or parallel (LP→HP = band-pass), and **four feedback paths** (self + cross) drive the circuit into self-oscillation with no input. A global envelope/LFO modulator runs 10 ms–10 s with four CV outputs (two positive, two negative); output clipping stages compress the runaway feedback.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/effects/feedback_lab.py` — **SP-160** (`FeedbackLab`)

- **DJW Audio DW-303** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-13)
  Tech: Free GPL-3.0 TB-303-style bass synth and step sequencer. It models the **signal path stage by stage from the original schematic** (oscillator, resonant ladder filter, envelopes, VCA) and then fits those models against hardware measurements of filter resonance, accent and slide behaviour. 16-step sequencer with separate pitch/time entry, per-step accent/slide/transpose, 64 patterns in four groups × A/B, seven chaining tracks, Full/Economy simulation modes, VST3/AU/AAX/CLAP/LV2.
  Replicable: PARTIAL — the 303 voice + accent/slide sequencing architecture already exists as `sound/generators/acid_seq.py` (SP-079); the product's actual value is its authors' **component models fitted to their own hardware measurements** (device variation, exact ladder nonlinearity), which are unpublished. Inventing "measured" curves would be fabrication.
  Musicom path: DONE — `sound/generators/acid_seq.py` (SP-079). No new code.

- **Trilling DARP** (soundonsound.com review, November 2026)
  Tech: Eurorack **dual arpeggiator**: two independent arps run around **one shared scale mask** edited on a small keyboard display; direction modes up/down/up-down/down-up/random; regular, dotted and triplet divisions; a **Wrap** value (1–64) sets each arp's sequence length before restarting; MIDI plus CV/gate outs.
  Replicable: PARTIAL — scale-mask + wrap-length + per-arp divisions are already `sound/synthesis/polyrhythm.py` and `sound/generators/arp1601_seq.py` (SP-144); the review is subscriber-locked past ~30 %, so the dual-arp coupling/latch rules are unpublished. No new code.
  Musicom path: DONE — existing arp modules.

## Items (one per gear finding)

- **Audio Damage Sandbender** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-11)
  Tech: 4-operator FM synthesizer where every operator carries its own waveshaper (triangle→sine→pulse morph) and can act as carrier or modulator; routings are user-drawn ("algorithm designer") with 10 factory algorithms, so the FM graph is arbitrary rather than a fixed preset list. Adds Xmod, Regen (per-op self-feedback), Detune, Spread and 32'..2' tuning per op, a wavefolder with symmetric/asymmetric/soft-clip modes, a sub oscillator, six noise types (white/pink/velvet/digital/crackle/metal), an 11-type stereo filter (state-variable, ladder, diode, Sallen-Key), and a **modelled low-pass gate** where brightness and amplitude are driven by the same control instead of a plain VCA.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/synthesis/sandbender_fm.py` — **SP-150** (`SandbenderFM`, `tri_sine_pulse`, `wavefold`, `svf`, `noise_bank`)

- **Anukari 1.1** (musictech.com, 2026-11-12)
  Tech: 3D physics-based synthesizer. 1.1 adds constructible resonator *objects* — strings, bars, plates, membranes, tubes, bells — plus a mouthpiece exciter that converts a construction into a wind instrument, and the Modulosphere, a sphere that floats through the virtual 3D scene and couples into whatever it touches. Energy transfer between objects is spatial, so moving a body re-voices the instrument.
  Replicable: YES (object/mode banks, spatial coupling, exciters; the product's GPU solver and 1000+ preset corpus are not replicated)
  Musicom path: `sound/synthesis/anukari_modal.py` — **SP-151** (`AnukariScene`, `ResonatorObject`). Complements the existing literal 3D solver `sound/synthesis/mass_spring.py`.

- **Bram Bos MIDGAR** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-11)
  Tech: Multi-stage ambience processor: a Phrase Delay captures short windows of input and replays them at different speeds with stereo placement and stutter/glitch repetition; then distortion; a Fluctuator for spectral + spatial movement; a stereo delay built on two simulated PT2399 CMOS chips (companded, band-limited lo-fi bucket-brigade-ish echo); then a shimmer reverb. Sections are independently adjustable and designed to interact.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/effects/phrase_delay_midgar.py` — **SP-152** (`Midgar`, `PhraseDelay`, `Shimmer`, `Allpass1`, `pt2399_delay`)

- **Unusable Engineering Currents & Nostalgia** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-11)
  Tech: Two circuit-modelled saw-core oscillators with adjustable component-tolerance variation, whose modulation scheme is *slot-based* rather than matrix-based: one system stores up to eight oscillator + waveform-mixer configurations, another up to eight filter configurations. Each system steps or sweeps its slots with its own pattern, at its own rate, host-synced or keyboard-responsive — and the rates can extend into audio frequencies, at which point the crossfade itself becomes a timbral modulator. Parameter locks hold individual fields while the rest move.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/synthesis/slot_morph_synth.py` — **SP-153** (`SlotMorphSynth`, `SlotSystem`, `saw_core`, `ladder_lp`)

- **Modbap Wax Theory** (soundonsound.com, 2026-11-11)
  Tech: Vinyl playback emulation where one "Side" control is physically grounded: ~51 cm/s groove velocity at the LP outer edge down to ~21 cm/s at the inner edge, and as velocity falls, tracing distortion, wow, bandwidth and surface noise all change together. Three formats (LP 33.3 / 45 / 78 shellac), three stylus profiles (HiFi / DJ / Worn) as tonal + pickup-resonance + tracking-loss shapes, a Stop control that brakes the platter (with the resulting pitch drop), and a Dust switch adding clustered ticks, a repeating scratch pop and bearing rumble.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/effects/wax_theory_vinyl.py` — **SP-154** (`WaxTheory`, `_asym_shape`, `res_peak`, `one_pole_lp/hp`)

- **quietformat CERNION** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-11)
  Tech: Wavetable synth (two oscillators, up to 256-frame tables, drawable waveforms with harmonic editing) whose modulation matrix gives **every assignment its own depth and response curve**, so one source drives different destinations with different curvature. Two arpeggiators: GILLA ARP, which generates evolving patterns that "maintain harmonic relationships between notes", and CLASSIC ARP, a conventional 32-step sequencer with per-step velocity and step length. Full MPE.
  Replicable: PARTIAL — the arpeggiator logic and the response-curve modulation matrix are replicated; the 256-frame wavetable editor / 11 effects / MPE plumbing are not (wavetable scanning already exists as SP-120 `waveshaper_voice.py` and `spectral_wavetable.py`).
  Musicom path: `sound/generators/gilla_arp.py` — **SP-155** (`GillaArp`, `ClassicArp`, `ModMatrix`, `response`, `consonance`)

- **UAD Cooper Time Cube Delay (native)** (soundonsound.com, 2026-11-12)
  Tech: Native port of the recreation of the 1971 Cooper Time Cube, which produced slapback by pushing audio through a garden hose — a small speaker at one end, a microphone at the other. Because the delay is a physical path, its character is time-of-flight plus tube resonance (odd modes at c/2L), wall absorption (HF dies faster) and dispersion; the unit has two hose lines with independent times and tempo sync, Treble/Bass/Color/HPF shaping, and stereo width coming from the two lines.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: `sound/effects/hose_delay.py` — **SP-156** (`CooperTimeCube`, `Hose`, `FractionalDelay`)

- **Aurally Sound Prism: Drums** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-09)
  Tech: Audio-to-MIDI plug-in for drum recordings: an ML engine detects nine drum components (kick, snare, closed/open hi-hat, crash, ride, high/mid/low tom) with timing and dynamics, so a performance can be edited, re-voiced or re-played through another kit. ARA 2 or insert workflows, local processing.
  Replicable: PARTIAL — the trained detector (weights + corpus) is unpublished; an invented classifier would be fabrication.
  Musicom path: n/a — **no code** (primitives exist: onset/transient detection in SP-085/SP-142; the synthesis side is SP-136 `drum_grid.py`)

- **Ableton Live 13 Step Sequencer** (soundonsound.com 2026-11-10, musictech.com 2026-11-12)
  Tech: New Live MIDI device with independent row lengths and per-row playback settings (evolving polyrhythms), per-step velocity, chance, repeat and pitch bend; plus Capture Automation (gestures → editable automation), ARA 2 support, expanded Racks.
  Replicable: PARTIAL (repeat of existing coverage) — row-length polymetry + per-step probability are already SP-078 `polymetric_grid.py` and SP-136 `drum_grid.py`; the only uncovered piece is a per-step pitch-bend lane, which is a MIDI-writer concern, not a synthesis method.
  Musicom path: n/a — **no code**, gap noted

- **Oxo Synth2** (synthtopia.com, 2026-11-09)
  Tech: Free open-source recreation of Daichi Laboratory Synth1 for macOS/iPad: 32-voice VA, 2 oscillators + sub, detune / FM / ring mod / hard sync / noise, five filter types (LP12, LP24, HP12, BP12, LPDL) with saturation, key tracking and velocity, amp/filter envelopes plus a mod envelope, two LFOs. Rebuilt by measuring ~3 GB of the original's output and modelling each parameter individually.
  Replicable: PARTIAL (repeat of 2026-11-09 verdict, new URL) — parameter-exact reproduction depends on the measurement corpus and Synth1's unpublished filter nonlinearity; the architecture is already covered by `polysynth.py`, SP-120, SP-150.
  Musicom path: n/a — **no code**

- **No-code batch:** Sonura Flow (text→loop ML), UVI Twin 88 Keys, Spitfire Eternity, MNTRA Living Sky, Freqport FreqLift, UAD Capitol Studios, Haloplane (MusicTech/Synthtopia 2026-11-10..12) — ML weights, sample/IR content or hardware; relevant DSP layers already exist (`room_reverb.py`, SP-092, SP-032).

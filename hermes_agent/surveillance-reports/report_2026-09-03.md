## Items (one per gear finding)

- **Native Instruments SuperStarSaw** (musictech.com review, 2026-08-20)
  Tech: A.G. Cook collaboration supersaw synth. Replicated logic: **two independent "swarms" of 16 detuned saw oscillators** per patch — detune is an alternating ladder (1, -1, 2, -2, ...) normalized and scaled by a Spread control; per-oscillator **stereo pan spread** (equal-power) and **amplitude drift** give the swarm its breathing chorus. **Harmony engine**: every oscillator's pitch can be quantized into a chosen scale (incl. Super Locrian, Dorian #4, Hirajoshi) or a chord shape (maj/min/sus/maj7/min9/add9/power...) — at small spread the swarm locks onto chord tones, at wide spread the quantized oscillators wrap into neighbouring octaves forming tense tone clusters. **Morph pad**: a 2×2 XY surface whose four corners hold full parameter snapshots, blended bilinearly (with harmony crossfading on the 0.5 threshold) — the product's signature gesture controller.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: sound/synthesis/supersaw_swarm.py (`SupersawSwarm`, `SwarmSnapshot`, `MorphPad`) — SP-033

- **UVI Thorus XT** (soundonsound.com, 2026-08-31)
  Tech: Dual-engine chorus. Replicated logic (analogue BBD side): **bucket-brigade clocked chorus** with per-voice LFO phase offset and rate spread, per-voice **hiss injection** that swells with the sweep, **alias filtering** (pre/post lowpasses around the clock path), **compander encode/decode** (soft-envelope expansion before the delay line, inverse gain after — mismatch pumps), a **morphable 1..8 voice** architecture (fractional voice counts crossfade), and clock-rate control. Stereo decorrelation via two independent ensemble instances + mid/side width.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: sound/effects/bbd_chorus.py (`BBDChorus`) — SP-034

- **Groove Synthesis 3rd Wave Shimmer Verb** (soundonsound.com, OS 2.0a, 2026-08-25)
  Tech: Reverb whose feedback pitch-shifts **in fractions of a semitone across ±2 octaves** (not just octave steps). Replicated logic: **SOLA pitch shifter** (resample by 2^(cents/1200), overlap-add Hann grains at original spacing → length-preserving fractional transposition) on the recirculated tail of a comb+allpass tank; **energy-normalized recirculation** makes the round-trip loop gain exactly rev-time-controlled (stable at any resonance); mod-matrix-style destinations (rev time / pitch amount / filter cutoff) plus a slow LFO wobble (±12 cents) on the live shift. Verified: +1200c steady-state 440 Hz → dominant 880/1760 energy; +250c → 528 Hz; −700c depletes the 440 band.
  Replicable: YES
  Musicom path: sound/effects/shimmer_reverb.py (`ShimmerVerb`) — SP-035

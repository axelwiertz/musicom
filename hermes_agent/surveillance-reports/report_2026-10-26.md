## Items

### 1. Blinksonic Holodrift (MusicTech, 2026-10-22)
Tech: Combined granular + spectral instrument. Up to 8 slots, each a full granular engine (samples, loops, live input) with grain size/scatter controls; a "Journey" control morphs between the slots (auto, manual, LFO or stepper); the summed signal then hits a spectral engine that decomposes it into partials, applies one of 15 spectral effects, and resynthesises, followed by more shaping sections plus a sampler/slicer. €119 intro (full €159).
Replicable: YES
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/effects/spectral_journey.py` (SP-126)

### 2. Teenage Engineering OP-XY "drumline" (Synthtopia, 2026-10-23)
Tech: OP-XY OS 1.2.0 drum synthesizer with ten models (kick, snare, hats, congas, shakers, clap…) each exposing four dedicated parameters plus sub-parameters, a redesigned attack/length envelope, a `funk` parameter that introduces rhythmic variation, and per-hit randomisation. Also a "dynamic filter" = high-pass + low-pass + compression + drive in one block, 8-channel USB audio, detune.
Replicable: YES
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/generators/drumline.py` (SP-127)

### 3. Sample Logic Quadra (Synthtopia, 2026-10-22)
Tech: Morphing multi-effect: four complete effect chains (up to four of 22 processors per corner) placed at the four corners of an XY pad and blended by a single gesture; motion engine with Manual, Auto, Draw-path and tempo-synced 16-step modes with swing; 630 presets, 81 convolution IRs, A/B undo. $99 (intro $69).
Replicable: YES
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/effects/quadra_morph.py` (SP-128)

### 4. Teenage Engineering OP-1 alternate firmware — "Keys" engine (Synthtopia, 2026-10-24)
Tech: Free alternate firmware for the OG OP-1 (built on firmware 246) adding a Keys synth engine with three models — tine, reed, clav — plus chord mode and scales, a chord-recording piano-roll sequencer, six new effects with their own screens, a new LFO, shift latch and MIDI CC on almost every knob.
Replicable: YES (the three key models + chord/scale mode; firmware/UI/effects not)
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/synthesis/keys_engine.py` (SP-129)

### 5. Knobula Drum Farm — "Player" flipper (Sound On Sound, Oct-2026)
Tech: 24 HP Eurorack drum module: a 16-sound drum synth built from virtual-analogue, spectral and physical-modelling algorithms, an SD sampler with 16 ROM + 16 user banks, and two resampling "Players". Player 1 holds 16 grabbed slots; Shift + slot knob selects one of 16 flip modes that reorder/reverse/ratchet/gate the slots in real time, and the slot selector can be modulated. Sounds can be sampled through the two-layer FX and re-flipped.
Replicable: YES (flip engine + grab workflow; hardware/FX/ROM banks not)
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/generators/sample_flipper.py` (SP-130)

### 6. Denormal Guardrail (MusicTech, 2026-10-22)
Tech: MIDI transformer that maps white keys to in-key chords and black keys to passing tones/extensions; Exact or Compact chord spacing, octave shift, randomisation; performance tools (strum via pitch wheel, arpeggiate via mod wheel); every input captured into a Performance List for dragging into a DAW. €39 launch.
Replicable: YES
Musicom path: DONE — `sound/generators/key_chord_transformer.py` (SP-131)

### 7. Roland JUPITER-08 Boutique (Sound On Sound, 2026-10-22)
Tech: Boutique recreation of the 1981 JUPITER-8: default 8-part two-voice architecture with a 32-voice option, refined voice allocation/pitch, 2-part 64-step poly sequencer, arpeggiator, 17 effects including chorus, five reverbs with shimmer. $549.99.
Replicable: PARTIAL
Musicom path: hardware; voice architecture already in `polysynth.py` / `memorymoog_synth.py`, sequencer in `poly_arp_seq.py` (SP-108), shimmer in `shimmer_reverb.py` (SP-035). No new code.

### 8. EastWest Hollywood Scoring Tools (Sound On Sound, 2026-10-06)
Tech: Scoring package with 164 EastWest/Sonuscore instruments (Orchestral 57, Synths 43, Band 20, World 34, Choir & Percussion 10) and a "Scores" system of 300 composer-designed multi-instrument patches / 1000 presets containing layers, textures, effects and controls. $299 pre-order.
Replicable: PARTIAL
Musicom path: sample-library content + hand-authored score templates; distribution logic already in `auto_divisi.py` (SP-112) and `voice_allocator.py`. No new code.

### 9. uJAM Detunicorn (Sound On Sound, 2026-10-23)
Tech: Free hypersaw synth: two supersaw oscillators plus a sub oscillator, up to four detunable unison layers spread across stereo, an octave pitch envelope with adjustable decay ("Bend"), HF emphasis ("Bite") and a large spatial reverb ("Infinity").
Replicable: PARTIAL
Musicom path: `supersaw_swarm.py` (SP-033) covers detune/unison/sub; shimmer/FDN reverbs cover the space; the bend is an envelope on oscillator pitch. No new code.

### 10. Areal SR1 (Sound On Sound, Nov-2026)
Tech: Monitoring headphones with four drivers per earcup (one full-range aimed at the canal, three handling >500 Hz placed above, in front of and behind it) combined with proprietary Acoustic Image Mapping, fed by a 12-channel USB controller with Stereo/5.1/7.1.4/Upmix modes. Reviewed 5/5.
Replicable: PARTIAL
Musicom path: multi-driver crosstalk cancellation is hardware + proprietary filters; binaural rendering already in `binaural.py` (SP-021). No new code.

### 11. Winzz R-30H HD81 (Sound On Sound, Nov-2026)

Replicable: PARTIAL
Musicom path: valve-style nonlinearity ≈ `topology_distortion.py` (SP-069); cab IR convolution ≈ `ir_designer.py` (SP-099) + the FFT convolution helper in `reverse_reverb.py`. No new code.

Release planned next year.

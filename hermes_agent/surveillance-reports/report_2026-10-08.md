### 1. Laboratory 0 BLOT — Experimental Modal Synthesizer
- **Source**: Synthtopia (2026-10-06)
- **Tech**: Geometric modal resonator network where the shape of a screen lump IS the patch. Each piece of the lump is a modal resonator: eight damped oscillators tuned by size with just-intonation ratios (1, 1.125, 1.25, 1.333, 1.5, 1.667, 1.875, 2.0). Small pieces ring higher, sizes settle on just-intonation ratios of MIDI root. Mode spread/brightness/ring time follow piece shape+heat. Excited by: slow breathing noise, strikes, friction (sweeping), and droplets (short bursting tones when hot). Pull out a tendril → plucked waveguide string (Karplus-Strong). Press a dent → formant filter/mouth (vowel position). Proximity pieces ring-modulate each other. Heat stretches modes and drives multiband saturation. 8-line feedback delay network memory.
- **Replicable**: YES
- **Musicom path**: sound/synthesis/modal_blot.py (SP-101)

### 2. Hermetic Modular Capicola — Real-Time Time-Stretch Eurorack
- **Source**: Synthtopia (2026-10-06)
- **Tech**: 12HP Eurorack module using "Extrema Sampling" — converts 48kHz audio into a non-uniform stream of keyframes representing local peaks and valleys. Keyframe representation used for time stretching, slicing, nonlinear processing. Pinch and time controlled independently. Grain Size control for time-stretch, Quality control for keyframe density. ±12 semitones pitch shift. Threshold transient detection + slicing. True-freeze via Stretch control. Keyframe-domain nonlinear distortion. 2 envelope followers. Open-source project.
- **Replicable**: PARTIAL — Extrema Sampling keyframe detection algorithm is proprietary. WSOLA/phase-vocoder time-stretch conceptually covered by sound/effects/phase_vocoder.py (SP-026). Keyframe-domain distortion and transient detection not meaningfully faked.
- **Musicom path**: — (no new code)

### 3. Baby Audio HyperWarp — Granular Graph Sequencer
- **Source**: Sound On Sound (2026-10-07)
- **Tech**: Granular processing plug-in with graph-based sequencer. Breaks incoming signal into micro-sampled grains, reassembles with "sonic imprint" of 7 built-in effect profiles. Draw speed shifts, stutter steps, direction changes. 7 profiles: Clean Warp (crisp temporal ± pitch), Flange Warp (tight grains, squeaky), Degrade Warp (lo-fi), Filter Spam (per-grain filter motion), Harmo-Nicer (harmonic density with pitch-shifted grains), Fauxcoder (resonant harmonic vocoder-like), Lo-Fizzly (granular down-sampling). 8 controls + sequencer. $99 ($49 intro).
- **Replicable**: PARTIAL — 7 effect profiles are proprietary. Granular engine conceptually covered by sound/synthesis/granular.py + sound/effects/scale_locked_granular.py. Graph sequencer concept overlaps polymetric_grid.py (SP-078). No new code.
- **Musicom path**: — (no new code)

### 4. Kreuzberg Audio Raspel — Free Filter+Wavefolder
- **Source**: Sound On Sound (2026-10-07)
- **Tech**: Free plugin combining filtering and wavefolding. 6 filter types: LP2 (12dB/oct), LP4 (24dB/oct), LP6 (36dB/oct), HP2, HP4, BP4. 20ms crossfade when switching. Saturation at input of EVERY filter stage (up to 30dB boost), linear filter feedback (resonance stays stable under saturation). Auto gain compensation. Character wavefolder after filter: 0.1–0.3 = sub-bass presence on small speakers, 0.4–0.6 = metallic leads, 0.7+ = aggressive industrial. Modern/Vintage modes. 1x/2x/4x/8x oversampling. Zero-latency mono-maker (20–500 Hz). Time-aligned parallel Mix (no comb filtering). 14 automatable params, 50 factory presets.
- **Replicable**: YES
- **Musicom path**: sound/effects/raspel_filter.py (SP-102)
